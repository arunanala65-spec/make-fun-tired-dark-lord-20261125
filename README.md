A TypeScript program that writes a ~100-word villain monologue to `/output/entry.txt`: a dark lord who has burned three kingdoms and outlived two prophecies greets the latest hero at his door — and the tiredness lives in the draught, his knees, and a cup of tea he wants to finish first, while the crown, the map, the seal, the dungeons and the ravens are all quietly on offer. The villain never says he is tired; the room says it for him.

Referral: https://make.fun/r/ce51ph37

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
